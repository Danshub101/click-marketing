Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Click Marketing ("we", "us") provides real estate photography, videography and AI avatar marketing in Christchurch and Canterbury. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Booking and enquiry details: your name, agency, email, phone number, the property address, your package selection and any notes you provide.
  • Property access details: vendor or tenant contact details, lockbox codes and access notes, where you give them to us.
  • Avatar footage and voice: if you choose an AI avatar video, the short video you record of yourself (including your face and voice) and the identity verification you complete.
  • Website usage: general information about how visitors use our website, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, collected through analytics tools.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm, schedule and deliver your shoot and marketing media.
  • To arrange access to the property with you, the vendor or the tenant.
  • To create your personal AI avatar (digital twin) and produce the avatar videos you request.
  • To respond to enquiries, including Priority Partner Office enquiries.
  • To invoice and keep the business records we are required to keep.
  • To understand how our website is used so we can improve it.

We only use your information for these purposes, or for a directly related purpose you would reasonably expect. We do not sell your personal information.

Your personal AI avatar

Your avatar is only created with your consent, and you will complete a short identity verification with our avatar platform before it is built. In particular:

  • Your avatar is only used to create marketing videos that you (or your agency on your behalf) request.
  • We never use your avatar or voice for anyone else, or for any other purpose.
  • We delete your raw recorded footage once your avatar has been built.
  • Avatar videos are clearly labelled as AI-generated, in line with Real Estate Authority advertising guidelines.
  • You can ask us to delete your avatar at any time by emailing info@clickmarketing.co.nz. We will delete it from our avatar platform and confirm when this is done.

Property photos and video

Media we capture is for marketing the property. We avoid capturing identifiable people and personal documents, and we will remove or blur anything sensitive if asked. We only use property media in our own portfolio or promotion with the agent's permission.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our business. They only process your information to provide their service to us:

  • Formspree: receives and forwards website booking and enquiry forms to us by email.
  • Dropbox: stores avatar footage you upload, until your avatar is built.
  • HeyGen: our AI avatar platform, which creates your avatar and handles identity verification.
  • Google Analytics: helps us understand website usage.

Some of these providers store information overseas, including in the United States. We choose reputable providers with their own privacy and security protections. By using our services, you acknowledge that your information may be processed outside New Zealand.

We may also share information where required by law.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses analytics cookies to measure visits and bookings. These do not identify you by name. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

How long we keep it

  • Raw avatar footage: deleted once your avatar is built.
  • Your avatar: kept so it is ready for future listing videos, until you ask us to delete it. If we have not created a video with it for 24 months, we will delete it.
  • Lockbox codes and access notes: only used for the shoot and deleted once the job is complete.
  • Booking and invoicing records: kept for as long as required for tax and business record-keeping.

Keeping it secure

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including using password-protected accounts and limiting access to the information to what is needed to do the job.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email info@clickmarketing.co.nz and we will respond within 20 working days.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at privacy.org.nz.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
